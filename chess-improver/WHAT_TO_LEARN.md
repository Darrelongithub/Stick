## How it teaches

1. **Blind recall.** You see the position and your game move, never Stockfish’s answer.
2. **Fail to reveal.** A wrong move refutes your idea; only after the final failed attempt is the best move unlocked.
3. **Practice and re-drill.** Step through the PV, play the best move yourself, then recall missed positions blind.
4. **Spaced repetition.** Missed positions return first; two clean blind recalls master a previously failed mistake.
